# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in DSH Hub to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy: do not post another person's paths, credentials, session
  logs, or screenshots without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing
private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who
violate this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers.

This is a small local-first project; keep discussions focused and kind.
